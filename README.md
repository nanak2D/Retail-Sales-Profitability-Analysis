# Retail Sales & Profitability Analysis
## Project Overview 
This project examines sales performance, profitability, regional differences and discount patterns using Microsoft excel. The objectives is to identify loss making product category and transactions, evaluate financial performance and develop recommendations based on the results.
The analysis uses pivot tables, formulas, charts and financial dashboard to examine revenue, profit margins ,sales volume and product level performance.

## Project Objectives

- Evaluate overall sales revenue, profitability, and profit margins.
- Compare financial performance across product categories and regions.
- Identify loss making product subcategories and individual transactions.
- Examine the relationship between discount rates and profitability.
- Develop financial recommendations based on the analysis.

  
## Tools and Methods
- **Microsoft Excel:** Data analysis and financial reporting.
- **PivotTables:** Summarized sales, profits, and discount patterns.
- **Excel Formulas:** Calculated profit margins and transaction level financial metrics.
- **Charts:** Compared profitability across categories, regions, and discount rates.
- **Financial Dashboard:** Presented revenue, profit, profit margins, and sales KPIs.
- **Transaction-Level Analysis:** Investigated loss making Tables transactions and calculated implied costs per unit.

  ## Key Financial Findings

- **Total Sales Revenue:** $1,924,337.88
- **Total Profit:** $224,077.61
- **Overall Profit Margin:** 11.64%
- **Total Units Sold:** 25,268
- **Sales Records Analyzed:** 1,952
- **Most Profitable Product Category by Margin:** Office Supplies (16.24%).
- **Loss-Making Furniture Subcategory:** Tables recorded approximately $193,765 in revenue but a loss of $7,240, representing a -3.74% profit margin.

## Discount Analysis

- **5% Discount:** Lowest profit margin at -53.60%.
- **9% Discount:** Profit margin of -28.86% and approximately $9,637 in combined losses.
- **0% Discount:** Highest profit margin at 40.01%.

These findings relate specifically to the Tables subcategory. Profitability varied across discount rates, but the results do not establish that discounts alone caused the losses. Further investigation of product costs, sales volume, and individual transactions is needed to understand the differences.

## Transaction-Level Loss Investigation

A detailed review of Tables transactions with a 9% discount identified an individual transaction with a loss of approximately $2,946.

- **Row ID:** 24764
- **Order ID:** 91195
- **Product:** SAFCO PlanMaster Height-Adjustable Drafting Table Base
- **Quantity Sold:** 12 units
- **Sales Revenue:** $3,918.98
- **Transaction Loss:** $2,946.05
- **Loss Per Unit:** $245.50
- **Implied Cost Per Unit:** $572.09
- **Revenue Per Unit:** $326.58

The implied cost per unit of $572.09 exceeded revenue per unit of $326.58, resulting in a loss of approximately $245.50 per unit. The implied cost was calculated from reported sales and profit rather than directly observed cost records. The Product Base Margin field was also missing for this transaction. Management should verify product costs, pricing, and data completeness before determining the cause of the loss.

## Financial Recommendations

1. **Investigate regional losses:** Review product profitability, pricing, discounts, and operating costs in the South region, which recorded a negative profit margin of -4.04%.

2. **Review Tables profitability:** Examine product costs, selling prices, and discount patterns in the Tables subcategory, which generated approximately $7,240 in losses.

3. **Evaluate discount policies:** Assess discount practices alongside product costs and sales volumes, particularly for Tables transactions with negative profit margins. Test any proposed changes before implementation.

4. **Investigate high-loss transactions:** Review individual loss-making transactions, including Row ID 24764, to verify product costs, pricing, and missing data before making decisions.
